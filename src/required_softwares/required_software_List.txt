STPC-GA external dependencies
=============================

Required Slepian software:

  slepian_alpha-master
    https://github.com/csdms-contrib/slepian_alpha
  slepian_bravo-master
    https://github.com/csdms-contrib/slepian_bravo
  slepian_delta-master
    https://github.com/csdms-contrib/slepian_delta
  slepian_zero-master
    https://github.com/csdms-contrib/slepian_zero

Optional DDK software:

  GRACE-filter-master
    https://github.com/strawpants/GRACE-filter

Preferred layout:

  <STPC-GA>/external/<dependency-folder>

Alternative layouts can be passed to startup_STPC_GA as dependencyRoot or
selected with the STPC_DEPENDENCIES environment variable. Third-party
source trees are not redistributed in this repository.
